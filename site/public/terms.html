Recording other people

Scribe records your microphone and the audio your Mac plays, which on a call means the other people in it. Laws about recording conversations differ from place to place. In some places everyone in the conversation has to agree before you record. You're responsible for knowing the rules where you and the people you talk to are, and for getting consent when it's needed. Scribe doesn't announce itself to anyone, so telling people is up to you.
